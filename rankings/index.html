What the demo meters showed

Damage meters read frame by frame from BlizzCon demo streams: premade level 38 to 40 characters, about 30 players in five groups. Most rows show a class but not a spec, tanks and healers are mixed in, and no healing meter was ever on screen.

    How these estimates are made
    1 · Start

    Classic log data for each spec at the same level: top-10% parses. Those include Classic world buffs and consumables, which flatter melee.

    2 · Adjust

    Every Forever talent, spell, stat and racial change we can read from the client, weighed by judgement. Talent values past rank one are often estimates, and stat conversions and hit caps are unpublished.

    3 · Check

    Against the demo meters beside this: small samples, fixed premade gear, and player skill and pull size matter more than spec there.

    4 · Listen

    "Strong" and "weak" count what demo players said about each spec. Confidence rises only where data agrees; most specs are still low.
