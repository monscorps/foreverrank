Every dungeon set rebuilt

Dungeon sets: five bonuses, not four

Tier 0 and 0.5 now pay out at 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 pieces instead of the old 2, 4, 6 and 8. The pattern across all nine classes: a resistance opener, cheap stat bonuses early, a class proc at 4 pieces authored fresh for Forever, a resource engine at 5 pieces lifted straight from Season of Discovery's spell data, and mana regen or a stat block to close.

Wildheart Raiment, as the client pays it now

(2)+8 All Resistances.Classic spell
(3)+30 Attack Power, and spell damage and healing up to 18.Classic spells
(4)When struck in combat has a 5% chance to increase your movement speed by 10% for 8 sec. Once every 60 sec.Forever-authored
(5)Chance on spellcast to restore 200 mana, on melee attack to regenerate 20 energy over 5 sec, and when struck to generate 10 rage.SoD spell reused
(6)Restores 8 mana per 5 sec.Classic spell

The era chips are read from spell IDs. The 4-piece proc is spell 1302708, deep in Forever's own 1.2 million range. The 5-piece engine is spell 450608, a Season of Discovery ID whose internal name still starts with "S03". Blizzard is openly recycling SoD's set-bonus payloads where they fit and writing new ones where they do not.

Tier 1 splits by role

The new Hyjal-tier raid sets are role-specific sets, not shared ones. Druids get four: Grovekeeper Raiment for Restoration, Rage for Guardian, Eclipse for Balance, Ferocity for Feral. Warriors, Paladins, Priests and Shamans get one per role; pure damage classes get one. The internal bonus names spell it out, "1.60.0 - Item - Tier 1 - Druid - Guardian 4P Bonus - Expertise", and each set carries four bonuses at 2 through 5 pieces.

Nothing in the data suggests bonuses swap with your spec. These read as separate pieces of gear per role, the Burning Crusade way, not the modern dynamic way.

The shadow copies

The biggest thing the coverage did not mention: more than half the ItemSet table is duplicates. 278 sets sit entirely in the 199,000 to 239,000 item ID band, the Season of Discovery range, and still carry the old 2, 4, 6, 8 bonus layout. The live rework rides the original classic item IDs instead. Wildheart Raiment appears twice: once with classic IDs and the new five-bonus structure, once as an untouched SoD copy.

This matches everything else we have traced in the branch: Forever is built on a codebase that carries SoD's data wholesale, and what matters is what gets re-wired versus what sits inert. On the Database the SoD copies hide behind the era toggle, labeled, like every other leftover.

Six sets nobody can place

Blessed Plate, Stormcloth Regalia, Teachings of the Furbolgs, Rider of the Plaguelands, Partners in Crime, and Blessing of Kalimdor are new set names with no known source. Rider of the Plaguelands grants mount speed and something called Lash of the Dark Rider at 3 pieces. Partners in Crime pays out at 2 pieces twice, which smells like a duo levelling set. Blessing of Kalimdor grants Kalimdor Speed, whatever that turns out to be.

One honest caveat

The reworked dungeon set bonuses are in the client, but the reworked piece items are not in this build's item export. The set definitions point at item IDs that do not resolve yet. The bonuses are real; the gear that wears them is still being itemized, so drop sources and stats remain unknown.
