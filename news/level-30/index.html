Quest XP: half the bonus

On 1 October Blizzard halved the extra experience that dungeon quests pay on top of a normal quest. Players' QuestBank uploads show exactly how.

The rule the game paysmultiplier after the cut, from the one before

new = 1 + (old − 1) ÷ 2

A ×3.75 quest on a 1,000 XP base, before3,750The same quest now, ×2.3752,375

It hits every quest with a bonus multiplier, dungeon-typed or not. Blizzard 1 Oct

164quests paid what the rule says, within 5%, in quest windows and hand-ins QuestBank users uploaded on build 70170 and later. Four paid something else, and QuestBank now carries the game's own number for them.

Dungeon quest multipliers, before and after

Grey: as Wowhead Forever's quest pages showed them before the cut (read 29 September). Gold: the rule above.

The rule is Blizzard's wording turned into arithmetic; the 164 quests are what makes it more than a reading.

Dungeon kills: about 20% more XP

On 8 October Blizzard raised the experience from killing creatures in dungeons. Aggrend expects dungeons to level about as fast as good questing with both changes in. Blizzard 8 Oct

Party XP: your own level counts

The penalty for killing lower-level enemies used the highest level in the party. It now uses each player's own level, as Classic Era does. Neither change touches quest XP, so the multipliers above stand. Blizzard 8 Oct Inferred

QuestBank already plans with the cut, quest by quest, and learns the game's own number from every hand-in you upload.

Rage, Warrior and Druid. On 8 October Blizzard fixed how taking damage makes Rage Blizzard 8 Oct:

  • It was measured against your own health, so more Stamina meant less Rage. It now uses an expected creature's health.
  • Crits and crushing blows count, and absorbed damage no longer does.
  • It assumes 20 to 40% armor by level instead of 50%, so most Warriors and Druids should get more Rage from hits.
  • Demoralizing Shout and Demoralizing Roar now cause threat; the client gave every rank a threat effect.

How we read it

Every client number is a row that changed between two of the eight beta builds wago.tools serves for 70009 to 70291: talents, spells, items, sets, achievements, currencies and maps. Tooltips are rendered from each build's own tables by the resolver that writes the Forge's talent text. Item stats are read with the game's item budget. Server hotfixes are not in those tables, which is probably why the Fury and Protection tree rework shows up only in 70291, a week after Blizzard shipped it Inferred.

The XP rule was checked against what QuestBank users uploaded: quest windows and hand-ins on build 70170 and later. Blizzard tags link to the post they come from. Inferred tags are our reading and could be wrong; tell us on Discord if they are.
