ForeverRank datamine, beta client 1.60.1.69876, September 17 2026

The systems Forever is hiding

Blizzard keeps saying discovery is the point of Forever. The client backs that up: several complete systems sit wired in the data with nothing in the visible game pointing at them. Three stand out.

Comprehension, a profession for spell research

SkillLine 3012 is a full profession line named Comprehension. Around it: Study spells authored under fresh Forever IDs, and a family of Comprehend Scroll spells, also Forever-authored, pointing at learning something from scrolls you find. The shape reads as a research profession, level your understanding, comprehend what you loot. None of it is reachable in the demo UI. All of it is wired like content, not debris; the IDs are Forever's own 1.2 million range, not imports.

Transmog infrastructure, in a Classic+ client

ItemModifiedAppearance, the table retail transmog runs on, carries 16,657 rows in this build. That is not a stub; that is a populated appearance system in a game that has announced nothing about transmog. Whether it ships as retail-style transmog, a barber-shop cousin, or quietly powers something else, the plumbing is installed and filled.

Priest racials going cross-race

Classic's priest racials, Desperate Prayer, Hex of Weakness, Shadowguard and kin, sit in the trainer wiring without their old racial fences, class-masked to Priest across races that never had them. The demo spellbook confirms several of them trainable. Classic's most famous racial identity quirk looks deliberately dissolved.

Why hidden probably means intended

One unwired spell is debris. A named profession line with Forever-authored spells around it is a decision. Forever's stated design leans on discovering things the UI does not advertise, and these systems are exactly what that looks like from the data side: built, connected, and waiting. As always, nothing is confirmed until it is obtainable, and all of it can change before launch.

Method: SkillLine, SpellName and ItemModifiedAppearance tables from beta client 1.60.1.69876, wiring checked against trainer rows and class masks. Counts are exact for this build and will move as the beta patches.
