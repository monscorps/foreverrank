Build 70291:
Dalaran opens

Blizzard opened the City of Dalaran dungeon for testing on October 8. The client shows it was ready all along: the same map, nine boss rows and a level-28 tuning since the first beta build. What build 70291 did change is everything around it. The Violet Sorcerer's Vestments got their first set bonuses, low spell ranks were rebased, Penance and Thorns came down, and the Excavation Site's quest rewards turned green.
