ForeverRank datamine, beta client 1.60.1.70009 against 1.60.1.69876, September 25 2026

Build 70009: kept, cut, rewired

Forever is Season of Discovery's codebase, which is Classic on the modern engine, with Blizzard deciding piece by piece what stays. Four beta builds have landed since day one. Diffing the newest against the first shows that sorting in motion: SoD's rune spells being renamed and tuned into baseline kits, Forever's own early drafts being thrown out, and new systems arriving that nobody has announced.

How to read this

Every claim below comes from a row that changed between the two client builds. The chips say which era a thing's ID belongs to: Classic for original 2004 content, SoD for Season of Discovery's ID range, Forever for work authored for this game. That is the question everyone is asking of this branch, so it is the axis this piece is sorted on.

One number to throw away first: the diff shows 1,054 changed spell power coefficients. That sounds like a balance pass and is not one. Nearly all of them are coefficients reset from 1 to 0 on passives, food buffs and talent modifiers that never scaled with spell power. Not one spell in a class spellbook moved. It is housekeeping.

Kept and worked on: SoD's runes become the kit

In Season of Discovery, runes handed out spells for engraving. Forever gives several of those spells to classes outright, and this build keeps shaping them like baseline abilities rather than leftovers.

Primal Bite SoDMangle is renamed. The rune spell (ID 407995) now reads "Bite the target" under a new icon, Forever's own three copies were renamed with it, and Ferocity and Berserk were reworded to match. Druid, Feral. Lava Burst SoDBase damage 113 → 164 on the rune version, 179 → 196 on the Forever version. The Forge talent now reads 164. Shaman. Lightning Bolt SoDThe SoD-range Lightning Bolt variants were rebased: less damage per level, more base damage, tighter variance. Classic's own ranks 3 and 4 also gained base damage (35 → 45, 50 → 56). Shaman. Hot Streak SoDLasts 20 sec instead of 15. Wake of Fire's post-kill window grows from 20 to 30 sec. Mage, Fire.

The other kept system is the book hunt. SoD's Mage rune quest, forty books scattered across Azeroth, survives in Forever and is open to every class; hand books to your faction's library for rewards. That one gets its own section below.

Cut: Forever's own drafts

The surprise of this build is what left. Of 92 spells removed, 88 carry Forever IDs. These were never SoD leftovers; they were Forever's own early work.

  • An "Adaptive" armor system Forever. 24 armor pieces named for a hunt (Slimeslayer's, Beastculler's, Dinodestroyer's, Earthcrusher's, and healer versions Slimetender's, Beastmender's, Dinomender's, Earthweaver's), plus 12 "Adaptation" spells and 11 "Contribute Reagent" spells. It reads like a gear line that adapted as you fed it materials. All 47 spells are gone.
  • A slime encounter and a Devilsaur hunt Forever. Slime Nova, Slimecontrolled, Experimental Plague, Coagulate, Sludge Suppressor; an Enormous Tranquilizer Dart, a Spike Trap, Devilsaur Hide. Un'Goro themed and removed together with the armor.
  • Fruit consumables Forever. Shiny Green Apple, Sweetsour Grapes, Wayward Pomegranate, Tel'Abim Plantains, Flame Papaya and three kinds of Fruit Pulp are out of the item table.

Classic content left too. Classic Linken's Sword of Mastery and Spirit of Aquementas, the two rewards of Un'Goro's Linken chain, are gone, along with Tempestria's Frozen Necklace, the Frigid Ring and the Hardened Stone Band. With the Un'Goro drafts gone as well, that region looks mid-rebuild. Black Dragon Mail lost its boots and its 4-piece bonus, and the developers' test map, Development Land, was deleted.

Smaller things

  • Nightclaw Druids of Shadowgale Forest on Zephras Isle, the Skyborne starting island, became a real reputation: the eight classic races were added to it, capped at Exalted.
  • Kirin Tor reputation now starts at neutral instead of deep in the negatives, which fits a Dalaran you are meant to visit.
  • Talent resets are tracked by two hidden currencies, and their windows swapped: class talent resets now count per hour, Legacy tree resets per week. Expect Legacy respecs to be the rationed ones.
  • Undead Paladins no longer start with Holy Light. Humans and Dwarves still get it automatically; Undead get their own row, which is not granted at creation.
  • Five low-level items gained spell power, including Harvester's Robe, Buckler of the Seas and the Leggings of the People's Militia, with part of their Spirit or Stamina budget shifted over.
  • 66 new items, 64 of them quest items, which is how new quest chains show up before anyone plays them: a Titanic Keystone with four hearts named Ados, Modr, Golm and Neru, Menethil Statuettes, Mulgore Expansion Plans, Dragonmaw Armaments.
  • Armor kits got names, among them new Forceful kits (attack power) and Mystic kits (spell power). Potions were renamed (Demon Slaying, Beast Slaying, Elemental Purging) and retuned: Frenzy potions doubled, Spellblasting and Mender's potions up, Dragonfire and Elemental Purging down.
  • Eureka!, the proc from the spell research system, was cut down: its resource discount on your next damaging abilities is now a flat 10% for everyone, from 50% for Mages and Warlocks, 40% for Warriors, 20% for Rogues and 15% for Priests.

Class changes

DruidWrath hits about 50% harder on every rank: rank 1 base 10 → 15, top rank 61 → 91, with more per level too. Mangle is Primal Bite; Primal Fury is Blood Frenzy. PaladinHoly Strike hits harder on seven of its eight ranks and its cooldown drops from 12 to 10 sec. Improved Holy Strike and Crusade are removed from the trees. Holy Power's crit bonus now covers Holy Strike too. Sacred Arbiter 10% → 20%. Two-Handed Weapon Specialization 3% → 2% per rank. Vengeance now needs a non-periodic crit and stacks to 3 instead of 5. Twist of Light also cuts Seal mana costs by 20%. Righteous Fury drops from 90% to Classic's 60%. WarriorSlam's cooldown rises from 15 to 18 sec, and Improved Slam now takes 3 sec off it at 2/2, so talented Arms lands back at 15. Bloodthrill is Main Hand only but doubles to 20% at 5/5. Sunder Armor still strips the same armor, but its bonus threat was cut by about 80% on ranks 2 to 5 (top rank 1013 → 206). ShamanElemental Fury and Elemental Alacrity swapped tiers: Alacrity sits where Fury was and Fury moved down behind Call of Thunder. Rage of the Farseer now speeds attacks only, not casting. Flametongue and Windfury Totem were rebuilt from passive dummies into proper proc effects. WarlockLife Tap's health cost now scales with Spirit as well, so it costs as much health as the mana it returns. HunterStrider Kick adds 30% movement speed for 3 sec.

All of it is live on the site now. The Forge carries the new talent texts and the Shaman layout, and shows the two removed Paladin talents struck out in place, so older shared build links still line up. The Database has the new sets, items and spellbook text.

The library, and what comes after twenty books

Turn in 10 books and the Friend of the Library quest offers a neck: Scholarly Pendant or Erudite's Amulet. Turn in 20 and Greater Friend of the Library offers a ring: Philanthropist's Ring or Field Researcher's Loop. The book counting happens server-side, so no client table says what happens next. The item table has a strong hint, though.

The four known rewards share a signature: rare, bind on pickup, no level requirement, no sell price, one particular flag. They also sit in tight ID blocks. Two more items share the signature in the ID slots directly after the 20-book rings, and a third sits directly before the 10-book necks:

Item level climbs 25, then 40, then 58, and all three candidates carry no level requirement. If they are the next tier, that is a remarkable reward at a level-20 cap. This is an inference from IDs and flags, not a confirmation. The names lean Explorers' League museum rather than library, so they may belong to a sibling quest drawing on the same reward pool. The test is simple: turn in book 21 through 30 and see whether a third quest appears. If you try it, tell us in the Discord.

Mages get something more from the same hunt: the hidden Research profession, whose Study ability turns Light Feathers into spell scrolls. It is the spell research system the client has carried since day one, and we wrote about it then.
