Nightclaw Druids become a reputation

The druids of Shadowgale Forest on the Skyborne starting island: the eight classic races added, capped at Exalted.

Kirin Tor start neutral

Your standing used to start deep in the negatives. Now it starts at neutral, which fits a Dalaran you are meant to visit.

Respecs are rationed

Two hidden trackers swapped windows: class talent resets now count per hour, Legacy tree resets per week.

Undead Paladins lose a freebie

Humans and Dwarves still start with Holy Light. Undead Paladins now get their own row, which is not granted at creation.

Eureka! was cut down

The spell research proc's discount on your next damaging abilities is a flat 10% for everyone now, down from as much as 50%.

How we read the client
wago.tools · 140+ tables + the file list→build 69876 vs 70009 diff→tooltip resolver→check: reproduce the old site text→site + this article

Every claim comes from a row or a file that changed between the two client builds, pulled from wago.tools: over 140 tables, plus the full list of 1.42 million game files. Tables the older build exports without column names were matched by position and checked by near-total row equality. Tooltips are resolved from Spell, SpellEffect, SpellMisc, SpellAuraOptions and the Trait curve tables by our own resolver, which reproduces 414 of 470 Forge talents exactly; new text is only published where the old text reproduced. Eras are read from ID bands.

Server-sent items, most quest rewards among them, are in no client table, which is why the new set pieces have no stats yet. The library tiers come from Warcraft Tavern's coverage of the quests; the candidates past twenty are our own inference.
