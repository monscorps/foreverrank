ForeverProbe is part of QuestBank now

One addon instead of two. Since 3.6.0, QuestBank notes what ForeverProbe did for the site: the items the game shows you, the spells you have learned and the gear you wear and carry, with no names. ForeverProbe's pace bar, guild roster and /probe export are gone with it.

Get QuestBankFree. On Windows, QuestBank Uploader installs it for you and sends its notes in.

What to do

1

Get QuestBank 3.6.0 or newer from the QuestBank page. On Windows, download QuestBank Uploader and double-click its Install.bat: it installs or updates QuestBank in your game folders, and replaces the old ForeverProbe Sync, keeping your WoW folder and what it already sent.

2

Log in once with both addons on. QuestBank brings in what ForeverProbe kept (item tooltips, spells and gear, never names), then says in chat that you can delete ForeverProbe.

3

Delete the ForeverProbe folder from Interface\AddOns. From then on QuestBank's own saved file, QuestBank.lua, is the only one to upload.

Now in QuestBank

  • The tooltip of each item the game shows you, the spells you have learned and the gear you wear and carry: one reading per class and race, with no character, realm or guild names. The Database marks what players' games showed.
  • Only on the Forever client. Settings, Discoveries has the switch: Note items and spells you see, for foreverrank.com, on unless you untick it. Unticking forgets what it had noted.
  • It stays on your PC until you upload QuestBank.lua on the QuestBank page, or QuestBank Uploader sends it.

Gone

  • The pace bar, the /probe panel and its export.
  • The nemesis list, the guild roster snapshot and the trainer scans.
  • ForeverProbe Sync: QuestBank Uploader takes its place. Questions go to the Discord.
