Download and unzip. You get two folders: ForeverProbe, the addon, and Companion, the optional Windows auto-sync.

2

Drop ForeverProbe into World of Warcraft/_beta_/Interface/AddOns/ and restart the game. The pace bar appears; that is the whole addon install.

Feeding the guild too? Double-click Companion\Install.bat, paste the webhook from the guild Discord once, done. A ForeverProbe icon lands on your desktop; clicking it shows sync status and a sync-now button.

What you get

  • Pace bar: level, percent, XP per hour, time to level. The estimate is rested-aware, blends this level with your past levels, and hovers to show a mobs-to-level range plus how sure it is. Drag it anywhere; right-click hides it.
  • /probe opens one small panel: status, pace toggle, export.
  • Nothing else on your screen, ever. No chat messages, no popups.

What it does underneath

  • Snapshots your character at login and level-up: spells, gear, zone.
  • Notes what class trainers offer when you open one.
  • Keeps the quiet record future features feed on: your PvP nemesis list, a guild roster snapshot per session, professions and progression. Dormant until the site grows those pages.
  • Everything stays on your machine until you press Export and paste the text here. Read-only, no automation, nothing sent on its own. Terms-of-service safe by construction.

Run it as a guild

The beta is the one window where a guild can map Forever before everyone else plays it. Three habits, nothing else.

1

Everyone installs the same zip. Same three steps, one minute per person.

2

Play the beta like you normally would. The addon quietly notes learn levels, trainer offers and your levelling pace while you do.

3

Pick a lane: fully automatic with the Sync companion already in the zip (details below), or manual, typing /probe export and pasting in the guild Discord when asked.

The Sync companion, already in your download

  • The Companion folder in the zip holds one readable PowerShell script and its installer. It watches your ForeverProbe SavedVariables file and posts it to the guild's private Discord webhook whenever the game writes it, hash-checked so nothing ever sends twice.
  • Double-click Install.bat, paste the webhook once. It runs every 30 minutes in the background, and the desktop icon answers a click with status and a sync-now button. Uninstall.bat removes every trace.
  • The addon still never touches the network; the game cannot and does not send anything. The companion runs outside WoW, reads one file, and every line of it is yours to read.

What your exports teach the site

  • Real learn levels past 38, the demo's ceiling. Every trainer visit confirms rows the datamine can only guess at.
  • Which SoD-wired spells actually train, settling the biggest open question in the Database.
  • The XP curve, level by level, from real pace data instead of the Classic assumption.
  • Soul Engraving the moment anyone finds it live, plus items seen in the wild before they hit any list.

The deal

  • Every confirmed fact lands on the Database labeled as beta-confirmed, and the guild gets the credit line.
  • Nothing is collected without you pressing Export. Skip a week, nothing happens; there is no tracking to opt out of because there is no tracking.
  • More characters and classes covered means fewer question marks for everyone at launch. That is the whole pitch.
