Download and unzip. You get a single folder named ForeverProbe.

Restart the game. The pace bar appears. That is the whole install.

What you get

  • Pace bar: level, percent, XP per hour, time to level. The estimate is rested-aware, blends this level with your past levels, and hovers to show a mobs-to-level range plus how sure it is. Drag it anywhere; right-click hides it.
  • /probe opens one small panel: status, pace toggle, export.
  • Nothing else on your screen, ever. No chat messages, no popups.

What it does underneath

  • Snapshots your character at login and level-up: spells, gear, zone.
  • Notes what class trainers offer when you open one.
  • Everything stays on your machine until you press Export and paste the text here. Read-only, no automation, nothing sent on its own. Terms-of-service safe by construction.
