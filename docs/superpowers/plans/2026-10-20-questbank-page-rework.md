   chips + snap strip on phone, grid on desktop, tap to enlarge, one-line captions.
5. Data story: counter strip (quests planned 1-60; with Forever's own XP; Forever-only; seen in players' games) and

6. Share `#share`: one sentence, the Uploader, "Upload by hand" folded; privacy in one sentence (check against Sync

   facts, never names, reach the next release."
7. FAQ `#help` folded, 6-7 at 40 words max: out of date; Lua error (/qb errors); XP differs from the plan; offers a

9. Footer, one line: data credits (Wowhead Forever, the Forever client, CMaNGOS GPL-3.0, players' games), minimap art
