# Cute Auras: design (2026-11-23)

A standalone addon on the Cute page, built from QuestBank's camp and reminder engine (Camp.lua, Remind.lua). It owns
everything about buffs: the camp's, yours, the ones you give, a paladin's blessings, and what to put back after a
death or a Hand. QuestBank and Cute keep their other reminders; their camp and buff rows step aside while Cute Auras
is loaded.

The owner's message (2026-11-23), in order, and what answers each point:

1. Camp: campfire buffs, the oven, the sewing machine, the blueprints, which ones you can use at the camp near you.
   -> The camp icons ported (Sit, Light, Place, Eat, Sleep). Place now shows at any fire the game says is near you
   (Campfire Nearby), not only your own kit's. Blueprints and camp items show only when you can use them. The Iron
   Oven and Cookie's Feast, which need a Cooking Fire, join the camp data.
2. Cleaner buff notifications; today only out of a fight and a bit buggy.
   -> One bar. In a fight the bar stays frozen and the alerts overlay works (section "In a fight").
3. Items outside your level, class or race must never be suggested.
   -> An item is usable only when its tooltip has no red requirement line and its required level is yours or lower.
   Fixed in QuestBank's Use reminder too.
4. The right buff for the right player, by spec.
   -> Roles from the talent tree's point groups, shared by members who run Cute Auras, else the group role, else the
   class. Arcane Intellect and Wisdom only for mana users; Might only for melee; Thorns for the tank.
5. PallyPower inside the same UI, plus Hands: an icon when you give Freedom, Sacrifice or Protection, and when it ends
   the blessing they lost, to click.
   -> Blessing assignment per class and role, a grid to change it, the blessings on the bar with a flyout of members,
   Hand countdowns and restores on the alerts overlay.
6. Deaths and combat resurrections: the right buffer, not every priest or paladin, is told to buff again.
   -> Each client works out the same assignment from the roster and what members announced. A secure handler watches
   each owed member's death and return in a fight and offers the click.
7. One bar for buffs, the camp's baseline look; an overlay for warnings you can size and click, with animations such
   as a countdown for Freedom and c... (the message was cut off here; read as "and cooldowns").
   -> The bar (pattern D icons) and the alerts overlay with its own place and size.

Added the same day (the owner: "don't forget the Well Fed and potion buffs"): Well Fed inside dungeons and raids and
under two minutes left anywhere; the potions, elixirs, flasks and scrolls you keep up, learned by habit (used twice),
missing or under a minute left, where Settings says (dungeons and raids by default); two that replace each other are
one group.

## What the client allows (Forever 1.60.1, checked in research/wow-ui-source)

- Auras are secret in a fight (C_Secrets.ShouldAurasBeSecret). C_UnitAuras.GetUnitAuraBySpellID works for a spell
  whose aura is not secret (ShouldSpellAuraBeSecret). Cute Auras asks the client per spell and reads what it may.
- UnitIsDeadOrGhost, UnitIsConnected and UnitIsVisible are plain in a fight. UnitInRange is secret.
- A secure button's attributes cannot be set by an addon in a fight. A secure handler's snippets can, and a state
  driver such as `[@raid5,dead] 1; 0` runs them in a fight. The restricted environment has UnitExists, UnitIsDead,
  PlayerInCombat, SecureCmdOptionParse, frame refs, RunAttribute and RegisterAutoHide. Handles to unprotected frames
  fail in a fight, so nothing insecure can pass a unit in.
- Addon messages can be locked down (Enum.SendAddonMessageResult.AddOnMessageLockdown). Nothing depends on a message
  in a fight: every client computes the same assignment from what it learned before.
- Forever has one ChrSpecialization row per class; the spec is the talent tree's point group with the most spent
  (C_Traits.GetGroupDisplayInfoByTreeID and GetGroupCurrencyInfo, the skill line of each group).
- Forever's long blessings are Might (melee attack power), Wisdom and Kings, an hour each, with Greater versions.
  Salvation, Light and Sanctuary are not in the client. Blessing of Protection, Freedom and Sacrifice are blessings:
  one per paladin per target, so casting one removes that paladin's long blessing.

## The bar

One row of up to 12 icons, three groups left to right: camp (gold bar under the icon), yours (white), the group's
(teal). A left-click acts; right-click is Not now, Never, Settings. The camp and your own icons hide in a fight as
today. The group's icons stay, frozen as they were, with each flyout still opening on hover through a secure wrap.

A group icon is one buff you owe: its count is the members missing it in range, its word the next member's name. A
click casts on that member, or the group version when three or more of one subgroup or class lack it and you carry
the reagent. Hovering opens the flyout: one secure button per member it covers, with what is missing.

## Assignment

- Buffers are members who run Cute Auras and announced the buff. Members without it are left alone. A buffer who
  turned the buff off (the bar or its group part off, Nothing in this instance, the icon on Never) announces nothing
  for it, so the share-out gives it to another.
- Single buffs (Fortitude, Spirit, Intellect, Mark, Thorns, Earth Shield): only the buffers with the highest announced
  rank are kept; in a raid, subgroup s goes to buffer ((s - 1) mod n) + 1 in GUID order; in a party, the first buffer
  does all.
- Blessings: buckets are class and role. Each bucket has an ordered wish list (tank: Kings, Might; melee: Might, Kings;
  hunter: Kings, Wisdom; caster and healer: Wisdom, Kings; never Wisdom without a mana bar). Paladins sort by level,
  then GUID; each wish in order goes to the free paladin who knows it and knows the fewest of the wishes still open
  (a tie: the first in that order), so one who alone knows a blessing keeps it. A class split over two roles lines its
  buckets up where the paladins give both the same set, so Greater blessings fit the whole class. Overrides from the
  grid (the paladin for themselves, the leader or an assist for anyone) win; a clear is kept, stamped; equal stamps go
  to the higher setter GUID. Your own overrides and the peers' hellos are saved at logout and read back within the
  hour into the same group (most of its members in both).
- Your role and Swap for me are each character's, never the account's.
- Lower ranks: a buff on a member at less power (Data.lua's power per rank) than the rank you would cast on them counts
  as missing ("Low rank" in the list, after those with none); your own buff of a kind, or a habit's own spell, at a
  lower rank than you know asks for an Upgrade (its own Never, by the spell's name; Never on the Buff hides both).
- The group version (Prayer, Brilliance, Gift, Greater) is cast only at a rank that three or more of those missing it
  take at full power, each at their own level (it lands on them and is not weaker than their single rank), on one of
  them in range; else the single rank.
- From others: a buff you lack, or carry at a lower rank, that the share-out gives you (or the strongest member of the
  giving class not running Cute Auras, by level: Divine Spirit and Kings are trained in Forever, not talents) shows
  greyed in your part after the consumables, naming the giver, after 30 s out of a fight while the giver is online,
  alive and in sight (at once at a ready check, even with your part on Not now); 3 at most; no click.

## In a fight

- The alerts overlay keeps working. The rebuff handler holds, for each member you owe, a state driver on their death.
  When one comes back in a fight, they join a queue, and the rebuff alert (a secure button) is set to them and your
  first owed buff. Each click moves on. Hunters are left out (Feign Death reads as a death). After the fight the bar's
  own reading takes over and the queue is cleared.
- Hands: your cast of Protection, Freedom or Sacrifice (UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT's target, guarded for secrets) starts a
  countdown alert. At its end comes the restore alert. Out of a fight it is set to that member; in a fight it casts
  on your target or mouseover and says whom to target. The member's row in the blessing flyout glows too.
